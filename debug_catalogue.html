About Us

Survitec is a leading supplier of tools, machines, and equipment in Kenya and the East African region, serving businesses, DIY enthusiasts, and professionals in different industries. Located at the heart of Nairobi City, Kenya, we specialise in 3D printing materials, woodworking machines, metalworking tools, and quality power tools engineered for durability and performance.
